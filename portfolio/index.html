Neelima Monjusha Preeti

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Boise State University (Fall 2025–present), advised by Dr. Edoardo Serra. As a Research Assistant in the Artificial Intelligence and Security Lab, my research focuses on prompt optimization with memetic algorithms, agentic AI, prompt injection, and LLM metacognition.

I have served as a Teaching Assistant for the Web course for two semesters and am co-developing courses for the university’s Applied AI Master’s program. Previously, I worked as an AI Engineer at Era Infotech Ltd. and as a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Business and Technology (BUBT).
